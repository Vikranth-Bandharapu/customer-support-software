Who We Are

A Company Founded in the Trenches of Support Operations

OmniDesk was founded in San Francisco in 2021 by three former site reliability and customer engineering leads who spent a decade answering urgent 2 AM customer pagers.

We grew tired of sluggish software that treated customers like numbered tickets on an assembly line. We set out to build an operating system that gives support agents superpowers while preserving human warmth.

First Principles

Why Customer Support Matters More Than Marketing

Every dollar spent on growth marketing is wasted if a new customer encounters a broken workflow and waits 48 hours for a boilerplate canned response.

"Customer service is not a cost center to be minimized with robotic call trees. It is your most direct channel for product discovery, retention, and brand advocacy."

When customers experience prompt, intelligent help that resolves their pain on first contact, their lifetime value increases by 300%. That is why we engineer software that treats support as high-craftsmanship engineering.

Our Journey

From Late Night Hackathons to Global Infrastructure

The first version of OmniDesk was built over a grueling 3-month sprint in a cramped garage. Our prototype was tested by seven early-stage developer tools companies who were drowning in user tickets.

Within 60 days, their average resolution time plummeted from 4 hours to 18 minutes. Word of mouth spread through engineering communities, and by late 2022, enterprise Fortune 500 banks were trusting our platform.

Genesis

Born From Disillusionment with Legacy Bloat

Traditional helpdesk software was designed in the early 2000s for static email tickets and slow call centers. We asked ourselves a radical question: What would customer support look like if it was rebuilt from scratch for the era of instant real-time AI?

Legacy Tools

Clunky nested menus, slow page reloads, fragmented customer databases, and detached chat windows.

OmniDesk AI

Single synchronized inbox, sub-second keyboard shortcuts, automated SLA triggers, and unified CRM.

Agents love their tools, tickets close in minutes, and customers recommend your product to everyone.
