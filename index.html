Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers about features, pricing, and migration.

What exactly is a unified inbox?

A unified inbox brings all customer communications from email, live chat, social media (like Twitter and Facebook), and voice transcripts into a single, centralized interface. This means your agents never have to log into separate platforms to help customers, drastically increasing response speed and reducing context switching.

Do you offer data migration assistance?

Absolutely. We understand that moving years of historical ticket data can be daunting. We offer free, white-glove concierge migration services from all major legacy helpdesk platforms including Zendesk, Freshdesk, Intercom, and Salesforce Service Cloud. Your historical data, knowledge base articles, and user profiles will be securely transferred.

Is SupportX compliant with GDPR and HIPAA?

Yes. Security and privacy are foundational to our architecture. SupportX is fully GDPR compliant, SOC 2 Type II certified, and we offer Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) for healthcare organizations requiring HIPAA compliance on our Enterprise tiers. We feature robust data encryption at rest and in transit.
