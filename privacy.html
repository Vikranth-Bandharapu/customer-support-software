Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026. We are committed to safeguarding your personal data and upholding global privacy standards (GDPR, CCPA, SOC-2).

1. Information We Collect

When you access or use SupportX ("Stackly Inc."), we collect information to deliver seamless omnichannel customer support experiences. This includes:

  • Account Credentials: Name, work email address, phone number, and hashed authentication credentials.
  • Customer Communication Data: Ticket messages, live chat transcripts, attachments, and customer resolution metrics.
  • Usage & Telemetry: Log files, browser fingerprint, API request metrics, and error traces.

2. How We Protect Your Data

All customer communications and workspace data are encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit via TLS 1.3. We maintain continuous SOC-2 Type II audit certifications and operate multi-tenant isolation across our distributed cloud infrastructure.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or business data. We only share data with essential sub-processors required to deliver our core services (such as cloud hosting providers and payment processors like Stripe) under strict data protection agreements.

4. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

You have the right to access, export, rectify, or permanently delete your account data at any time. To exercise your rights, please submit a request to our data protection officer at privacy@supportx.demo or through our Contact Page.
