Services & Capabilities FAQ

What are the API rate limits on your plans?

Our standard plan allows for 1,000 API requests per minute. Pro plans increase this to 5,000 requests. Enterprise plans feature effectively unlimited API calls with dedicated infrastructure to ensure high availability for intense bulk operations and custom headless implementations.

Can I host the knowledge base on my own domain?

Yes, absolutely. Custom domain mapping (e.g., support.yourcompany.com) is available on all paid tiers. We also automatically provision and manage free SSL certificates for your custom domain to ensure your self-service portal is fully secure.
